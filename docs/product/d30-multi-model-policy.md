# D30 — multi-model policy

The risky graph keeps verification and publication deterministic. Its central
policy assigns Flash to bounded evidence packets and Pro to compact plans. The
evidence packet retains source identity and verbatim ranges; a summary cannot
be published. The planner's terminal choice is `{item,status}` only, and the
deterministic verifier binds it to the pre-existing cross-reference entry or
pointer.

The offline gate uses no LLM access key and no paid calls. It proves the public
Flash catalogue and price, structured-response request shape, cache-key
separation, semantic offset binding, keyless refusal, the actual cached route,
and an injected filing instruction that cannot alter policy or publication.
Each risky document gets its own 1 Flash + 3 Pro / $0.10 guard even when the
caller also supplies a shared process budget. Cached calls remain free.
