# 031 — D21 systematic escalation (2026-08-29)

## Prompt decision

The owner requested a bounded systematic escalation slice: classify failures,
retain strict primary spans, and publish only deterministically verified
alternative evidence without increasing paid-call exposure.

## Outcome

The classifier exposes the route and target items. Missing-primary shapes use
item-scoped regions anchored by a title or reference; overlapping regions stay
outside primary offsets. Cross-reference resolution suppresses escalation.
The routing envelope is also the sole five-stage inspector flow. A bounded
vision seam resolves at most two relevant relative images only against a
validated SEC Archives document URL, then sends those URLs with the already
verified item/region text to an image-capable committed model. It records live,
cache, or cached-test provenance, status, model, image count, and cost; strict
confirm/reject/null can gate evidence but cannot create offsets. Uploads and
fixtures visibly skip, and full-page rendering/OCR remain residual.

## Assumption → Eval contradiction → Correction
- Assumed: a parsed, bounded region was adequate evidence.
- Eval said: the D21 invalid-region case accepts in-bounds data with no
  title/reference anchor under the old verifier.
- Corrected: verifier requires a declared, verbatim title-or-reference anchor.
- Assumed: a global vision verdict could gate every proposed alternative.
- Eval said: two sampled images can neither reject unseen sibling items nor
  justify deleting their separately verified text evidence.
- Corrected: the verdict applies only to item codes whose regions contain the
  inspected images; unrelated siblings survive.
- Assumed: an `<img>` annotation plus SEC origin was sufficient resource proof.
- Eval said: blank and query-only sources resolve back to the filing HTML.
- Corrected: only non-empty common image paths under SEC Archives are eligible.
- Assumed: missing provider usage could safely default to zero.
- Eval said: that publishes a paid live call as free and weakens the shared
  budget record.
- Corrected: usage-less live responses are unpriceable and fail loudly.
