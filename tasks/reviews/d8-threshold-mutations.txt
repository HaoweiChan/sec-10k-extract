D8 / ADR-034 — THRESHOLD MUTATION PINS (post-change tree)
=========================================================

Each block mutates ONE constant in src/sec10k/validate.py and re-runs the
committed cases that pin that constant's band edge. A threshold whose green
range is wider than its measured band is unpinned; these are the runs that
show it is not (ADR-030 §f's precedent, ADR-027 §c's rule). The source is
restored after every block.

Note on the harness: the two COVERAGE_MIN edits are the same byte length as
the original, and CPython invalidates a .pyc on (int(mtime), size) — written
inside the same wall-clock second they left a stale .pyc and the mutation
silently did not run, reporting PASS. The harness drops
src/sec10k/__pycache__ between blocks. Recorded because a mutation test that
does not mutate is indistinguishable, in its output, from a threshold that is
genuinely unpinned.
